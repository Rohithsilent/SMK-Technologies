# SMK wordmark

## Files

- `smk-mark.svg` — primary SMK wordmark for dark backgrounds.
- `smk-lockup-dark.svg` — navy and gold SMK wordmark for light backgrounds.
- `smk-lockup-light.svg` — gold and warm-white SMK wordmark for dark backgrounds.
- `../favicon.svg` — compact SMK wordmark for the browser tab.

## Brand colors

- Deep navy: `#062B40`
- Warm gold: `#D6A547`
- Warm white: `#F4F0E8`

The logo keeps the SMK letters as its focus, with a graduation cap over the S and a rising student figure integrated into the M. It omits the company name and tagline. The SVGs have transparent backgrounds and can be used in print or web layouts. Keep their proportions; choose the gold-and-ivory version on dark backgrounds and the navy-and-gold version on light backgrounds.
